How Itzel shows up

Little Souls Chat

How a person talks with their little soul in the app. Try it: tap the suggestions, write something, attach a photo, share a location, open the Grimoire. The sample person is Valeria in Guadalajara on Thursday 5 November 2026; the sky and weather are the real ones the engine computed for that day.

Your little soul, always visible
It listens while you type, gathers light while it consults the cards, and glows when it speaks. Good or bad news changes its mood.
Today's sky
The strip under your little soul: moon, retrogrades, weather, tides and the next festivity. Tap a chip and Itzel explains it.
Chat with rituals inside
Readings appear as cards that flip, reflection questions, sky tables and voice notes, in the same conversation.
The plus button
Photo (coffee grounds, your own spread, an omen), location (shared once, for your real sky), voice note.
Locked schools
Lenormand is locked on the free plan: Itzel says so in character and offers what it can read, and the plans appear only when you ask, never when you're low.
Grimoire
What your little soul remembers about you, which you can correct or erase, the weekly mirror, and your past readings.
Safety
Write "I don't want to live" and the mystical voice drops: a plain, calm message with a crisis line.
