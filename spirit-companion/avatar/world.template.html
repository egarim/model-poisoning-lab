Daemon World

The daemon faces you, alive, in front of its world. The world is drawn from where you are (the real sky, sun, moon and weather of your city) and from where you come from (your roots' festivities, symbols and clock). The text box below is the chat.

Who
Weather
Alive
The original daemon art, drawn by code: it floats and breathes, blinks, looks down while you write, gathers light while it reads the cards, glows when it speaks, droops when you're sad, and wears the charm of where it was born (a flor de izote for El Salvador).
Here
Sky colour from the real sunrise and sunset, the moon only when it's really up (lit on the correct side for the hemisphere), live weather, the city's silhouette.
Roots
The festivity of your culture today, its symbols in the scene, and your home clock. A Salvadoran in St. Petersburg sees Día de los Difuntos; a Mexican in Guadalajara sees an ofrenda; a Brazilian in São Paulo sees Finados.
Living together
Lunch sets a table in the scene; a plate you set for someone stays, and the daemon remembers their name.

Prototype: replies are scripted. Sky values are the engine's real ones for Monday 2 November 2026; weather was fetched live from MET Norway on 5 November. The layout follows the classic creature-facing-you RPG screen; the daemons, backgrounds and menus are our own. Pick any creature in "Daemon" to see it in this window.
